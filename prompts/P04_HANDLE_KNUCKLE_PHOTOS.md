# P04 Handle, knuckle and battery pod from the reference photos
Open docs/reference/photos (IMG_2940.png, IMG_2941.jpeg, IMG_2942.jpeg). They show a carbon telescoping detector with a pivoting knuckle head mount and external cam locks, a crutch-style armrest and grip, and a side pod where a yellow control box sits.
Goal: redesign the upper shaft, elbow, grip, armrest cuff and battery pod of this project in that language: (1) crutch/forearm grip with the quick-swap 7S1P pack in the side pod; (2) Minelab-style knuckle for the head hinge (compare with the serrated 10-deg hinge in src/mech/yoke_v20.py); (3) external cam lock on the telescoping tube A/B pair; (4) keep the ribbon/umbilical routing and the harness (data/results/HARNESS_MASTER.csv) consistent; (5) recompute balance: head mass 2.22 kg, static torque 2.88 kg m baseline, target under 2.2 kg m; (6) fit checks against the real tubes (OD33/ID30, OD28.5/ID26, lower OD32/ID29), 18650 pack, PM1 and HB1 boards.
Deliver: generators in src/shaft, STLs, meshes checked, balance table, updated figure, and a list of what is guessed from the photos (dimensions are NOT in the photos: ask for or state assumed values).

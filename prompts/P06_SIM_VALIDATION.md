# P06 Validate and bound the simulations
Goal: make the numbers defensible. (1) Convergence: segment counts of the Biot-Savart/Neumann integrals; (2) Monte Carlo on tolerances (offset 0-0.5 mm, layer gap, litz area, L/R spread, FET Coss and C0G tolerance, coil self-capacitance 150-350 pF); (3) ground model; (4) finite turn-off convolution instead of a step; (5) non-spherical and magnetic-susceptible targets; (6) compare with analytic limits (axial field of a loop, mutual inductance of coaxial loops, sphere decay constant); (7) an independent re-derivation of the first-clean-gate with a different method.
Deliver: a table of every headline number with median and 5-95% range, the dominating assumptions, and a list of measurements that would pin each one down (docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md). Update docs/CORRECTIONS.md if anything moves by more than 10%.

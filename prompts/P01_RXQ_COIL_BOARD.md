# P01 SB1R-Q receive board generator and Gerbers
Goal: generate the 8-layer D228 receive board: QUAD-A on layers 1-4 and QUAD-B on layers 5-8 (rotated 45 deg), 24 turns each, no copper pour, 4 lead pads at r48.3 under the hub wall (angles from docs/DESIGN_STATE.md RX lanes), 3 screw windows r41, index key at 0 deg, fiducials.
Context: src/sim/sim_tanr.py and sim_quadbearing.py hold the idealised loop model (4 quadrant lobes at (+-50,+-50), radii 15-45; quad B = the same rotated 45 deg). src/pcb/gerber_out.py and router.py show the Gerber and via conventions. Board area is limited by the D229 recess; lobes must stay inside r114 (the idealised radii 45 at centre distance 70.7 reach r115.7: fix this and say how).
Steps: (1) design the lobe geometry (spiral or nested sectors) and the series connection pattern that gives the + - + - polarity and adds across the four layers; (2) generate copper polylines and via stitching; (3) generate Gerbers (copper, mask, outline, drill) with src/pcb/gerber_out.py conventions; (4) rebuild the geometry FROM THE GERBER POLYLINES in the mutual-inductance model and recompute: peak leak at turn-off for 0 / 0.1 / 0.5 mm offsets, bearing scatter, signal vs the previous model, L and R; (5) draw one figure per layer pair; (6) add checks to tests/regress.py; (7) update docs.
Acceptance: DRC-style self-check clean (min trace/space stated), L and R within 10% of 360 uH / 5.4 ohm or an explanation, leak within a factor of 3 of the idealised result. Mark everything that is modeled only. Do not call it fabricated-ready until an external CAM viewer opens it.

## Coil windings: no pour

Decision: **no pour on the windings: lock the turns with a thin SYN bead at the ends only and verify on the bench**. Rule: only a fill that keeps the quiet time >= 0.10 us before the first gate in the whole phi range.
The coil self-capacitance (250 pF of the 500 pF node, ASSUMPTIONS 1) scales with the fill as C (1 + phi (er - 1)); the quiet time comes from the transmit model (boost, 24.5 V).

Even the lightest syntactic fill at the low phi bound loses the first gate in the model, so the windings stay unpotted; thin SYN beads at the winding ends lock the turns, and PT2 measures the real C_self and quiet time first.

## Coat only

- L0 SB1R-QA / QB receive boards: RX lobes: a pour adds capacitance to the receive coils (sbq_damp model); coat only
- L5 CB1D: Pico 2 W antenna and 10.2 mm parts under LB1; coat + wire staking
- L6 LB1: LEDs shine through the pixel holes: clear coat only
- L8 cap / HTA board: final bond not specified; coat
- AU1: speaker, jack, selector leads and the SWD pads stay free; coat + staking

## No fill (keep-outs)

- grip channels (U2): cords move with the trigger and the housings
- shaft tube B twin cavity: collapsible shaft: the cords slide
- battery pack U7 and dock U6: removable pack; Li-ion cells need a vent path
- spine duct and comb windows (head): FFC passage (masked insert)
- programming pads (AU1 JSWD, CB1D JDBG): clip-on probe access
- moving parts: trigger, dial, power button, latch: mechanisms

## Checks

- Eddy rule: every filler is an electrical insulator (True); no conductive filler forms a closed loop (CI is an insulated powder in silicone).
- Vault temperature: the unpotted thermal model's worst case is 50.6 C at 40 C ambient (limit 80 C); the thermal model is unpotted air; replacing air (0.026 W/mK) by GEL (0.15-0.2) only adds conduction, so the unpotted worst case is an upper bound.
- High voltage: the NPM hot zone wets DRAIN1 / CLAMP1 and keeps the 1.0 mm board clearances; void-free pours (vacuum degas) are required (PT6, PT7).

## Owner proposal: one epoxy matrix (epoxy + 3M iM30K glass bubbles + BN / alumina + fumed silica), evaluated 2026-10-28

Generated by `src/sim/potting_matrix_eval.py` into `data/results/potting_matrix_eval.json` (pipeline step `potting-matrix`). MODELED; constituent values ASSUMED except the 3M iM30K data (DATASHEET_LOG, ASSUMPTIONS 44).

Proposed mix (41 % epoxy, 45 % iM30K, 12.5 % BN, 1.5 % silica): thermal conductivity 0.313-0.346 W/mK, er 2.31-3.08, density 1.003-1.093 g/cc. 3 W/mK needs about 34 vol% BN or 37 vol% alumina with no bubbles.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| iM30K glass bubbles: real 3M product, 0.6 g/cc, high crush strength | CONFIRMED | 3M table: 0.60 g/cc (0.57-0.63), 28,000 psi 90 % survival (data sheet Rev C: 27,000) |
| BN / alumina at 10-15 vol% gives > 3 W/mK | NOT SUPPORTED | Bruggeman with 45 % bubbles: 0.313-0.346 W/mK (BN), 0.312-0.344 (alumina); 3 W/mK needs about 34 vol% BN or 37 vol% alumina with no bubbles |
| the mix is invisible to the TX / RX coils (mu_r = 1) | HALF TRUE | magnetically yes; electrically no: er 2.31-3.08 raises the coil capacitance; injected through the windings the quiet time misses the first gate in every case (see coil) |
| shrinkage < 0.1 %, zero drift, 100 % moisture barrier | UNVERIFIED / overstated | no product data sheet; a rigid epoxy also loads the precision parts (AB1 offsets) and solder joints by CTE mismatch: the soft gel was chosen there for that reason |
| microballoons damp vibration and remove bump false targets | PARTLY | rigid potting stops winding / lead motion (the real microphonic source); glass bubbles in a rigid epoxy add little damping; bump signals from coil-to-ground motion are not removed by any potting |
| BN removes hot spots on the driver chips in the head | DOES NOT APPLY | the head has no driver: the FETs and clamp are in the neck module (boost about 3.9 W there); the head dissipates 0.67-1.38 W and peaks at 50.6 C at 40 C ambient (limit 80 C) |
| a single compound filling the whole head with conductive filler would ruin detection | CONFIRMED | eddy rule (CLAUDE.md 5): no conductive filler anywhere |
| paint the circuit cavity with grounded conductive shield paint | ONLY IF SLIT | a closed painted wall around the axis is a closed loop (eddy rule 5): see paint; graphite paint decays too fast to matter but shields little; a slit / comb pattern with one ground point keeps the electric shielding and breaks the loop |

31. **OPA2211 input common-mode range on the 5 V rail (Step 5 finding)**: for VS < +-5 V the range is (V-) + 2 V to (V+) - 1.4 V = 2.0-3.6 V (TI SBOS377L); VCM = VREF / 2 is about 2.05 V (VREF 4.096 V assumed), so a negative input excursion of more than about 0.05 V leaves the range. 32. **Trigger magnet versus the Hall switch spread (Step 5 finding)**: DRV5023BI needs >= 24 mT at rest and <= 3 mT pressed at the sensor (worst case over -40 to 125 C); the trigger geometry (4-8 mm rest gap, >= 2 mm travel) gives about a 3.4x drop for a dipole, so only typical thresholds (14.5 / 6 mT) are met. Choose the magnet and either a closer rest gap, more travel or a first-article screen; add a magnet-field check to upper_check.py.

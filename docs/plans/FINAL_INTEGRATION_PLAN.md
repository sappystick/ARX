# Final integration plan (2026-10-28): software, firmware, manufacturing margins, housing and potting, ferrite hood

Status of everything here: MODELED / tool-checked. Nothing is built. Each work package ends in a script, a JSON result, a regression row and, where a number
moved, a CORRECTIONS row (CLAUDE.md rules 2, 3, 4, 8). A package is "done" only when its acceptance line is met by a check, not by a statement.

Order: packages that need no hardware and remove the largest unknowns first (P1-P4), then the firmware/DSP chain that depends on them (P5-P7), then the
first-article loop that turns models into measurements (P8). Started in this session: P1 and P2 (results at the bottom).

## P1. Manufacturing margin audit of every board (started)
Why: DRC proves each board meets its rule; it says nothing about how far above the rule it sits. Etch tolerance, registration and mask slivers eat margin, so the
fab risk is set by the few places that sit exactly at the floor.
Method: `src/pcb/fab_margin.py` reads each released .kicad_pcb with pcbnew, builds the copper of every net per layer (tracks, arcs, pads, vias, zones) as shapely
polygons and measures, per layer: minimum copper-to-copper gap between different nets, the count of places within 0.02 mm and 0.05 mm of the floor, copper to
board edge, drill to drill, annular ring of every via and plated hole, and the flyback (HV) nets' minimum gap to anything else. Floors: docs/design/FAB_RULES_VERIFIED.md
(clearance 0.16, track 0.15, via ring 0.15, PTH ring 0.18, drill to drill 0.41, edge 0.5, HV 1.0 mm).
Accept: every measurement >= its floor (the gate, same as DRC); a margin table per board in data/results/fab_margin_audit.json; every location within 0.02 mm
of the floor listed with coordinates, so each can be widened in the generator where room allows (never by loosening a rule).
Next step after the audit: per board, a "widen where free" pass in the router/generator for the listed hot spots, re-run, report the moved minimums.

## P2. Exact potting and fill volumes from the meshes (started)
Why: POTTING_SEQUENCE.md quotes box estimates (NPM 28 + 58 cm3 gross, absorber cell upper bound 31 cm3). Resin is ordered, mixed and degassed by volume; a
40 % error means voids or waste.
Method: `src/mech/potting_volumes.py` computes the free volume of each poured cavity as exact mesh booleans (manifold3d): cavity envelope minus the printed
part minus every board and component body that sits in it (PB1R bodies from the generated board model; the absorber cell from the v5 cylinder, septum and
CB1D slot geometry), split into the alumina hot zone and the syntactic remainder by the dam plane. Mass = volume x density (densities stay ASSUMED until a
resin datasheet is on disk). Also reports the fill-port to vent path (highest point of each cavity vs the vent height) so air cannot be trapped.
Accept: volumes in data/results/potting_volumes.json, POTTING_SEQUENCE.md quoting them with their method, a regress row that the volumes are positive,
smaller than the envelope and that every cavity has a vent at its highest point.

## P3. Ferrite hood placement accuracy
Why: the hood adds +10-18 % field (hood_axi.py) and is a magnetic part next to a nulled receiver; tile position errors change both the gain and the RX leak.
Method: (a) sensitivity of the axis field and footprint to uniform radial / axial tile offsets and to the butt-joint gap (axisymmetric hood_axi with
perturbed rings), giving dGain/dmm; (b) the tile cut list tolerance and the carrier pocket clearance from those slopes for a gain error <= 1 %;
(c) a printed placement jig (generator + fit check) that fixes each tile against two datums; (d) bench: field with and without tiles (ASSUMPTIONS 8).
Accept: tolerance table (mm per 1 % gain), jig STL with closed-mesh and fit checks in regress, hood first-article procedure in BENCH_FIRST_ARTICLE.

## P4. Housing, cavity and compartment integration
Method: extend interference_audit.py from "no overlap" to "clearance map": for every adjacent pair, the minimum gap measured on the meshes, compared with the
print tolerance (0.2 mm FDM, ASSUMED until printing trials, OPEN_ITEMS 10); sealing faces (skirt, cap, roof) checked for continuous contact bands; every cable
passage checked against its cable bundle area (fill ratio, as the TX lead channel row); HEAD_SKIRT moved to a current generator (OPEN_ITEMS 39).
Accept: clearance map JSON, zero pairs under the print tolerance unless designed as press fits (listed), skirt regenerated by a pipeline step.

## P5. Firmware timing and integration (RP2350 on CB1D)
Method: one timing model `src/sim/fw_timeline.py` that builds the per-pulse schedule from the existing results: on-time from TX_ONTIME_LUT (V measured each
burst), turn-off and quiet time (tx_vbus), blank release, receive gates, AD7389-4 conversion and 2-wire readout times (datasheet on disk, ad7389_readout.py),
LED and display slots, NTC / ISENSE / FAULT polling, watchdog; prove every slot fits the 400 / 600 / 800 Hz frames with margin and that no SPI or LED edge falls
inside a receive gate. Output: a cycle table the firmware compiles from (header generated from the model, so firmware and model cannot drift).
Accept: zero overlaps, margin per frame >= 10 %, generated C header checked into the firmware tree, regress row on the timeline.

## P6. DSP in Python first (OPEN_ITEMS 5)
Method: synthetic receive records from the models (coil response, leak, ground term, noise at 1.7 nV/rtHz, gold sphere tau = mu0 sigma a^2 / pi^2), then the chain:
air-template subtraction, gate integration, ground balance (two-tau cancellation), matched-filter tau estimate, quadrupole bearing 0.5 atan2(B, A) + 45 deg,
depth estimate from the field model; Monte Carlo over tolerances (OPEN_ITEMS 6) so every depth number carries an uncertainty.
Accept: confusion matrix and depth error versus truth on the synthetic set; the same code then runs on bench records unchanged.

## P7. Ground model and tolerance Monte Carlo (the gap named in the performance review)
Method: viscous (log-uniform tau) magnetic soil response added to the target model; Monte Carlo over receive-board offset (0-0.5 mm), layer gap, etch width,
self-capacitance; depth reported as P10 / P50 / P90 in air and over two soil classes.
Accept: depth table with uncertainty replaces the noise-only rows; CORRECTIONS row if any published depth moves.

## P8. First-article loop (needs the owner: B2, B10)
Fabricator DRC and CAM viewer on every board (U-FAB-DRC, U-CAM-VIEW), then the bench procedure: TX first article, receive leak and ring-down, hood with and
without tiles, potting by weight; every measured value goes back into the model it checks, and the model is re-run before the next build.

### P2 result (src/mech/potting_volumes.py -> data/results/potting_volumes.json, pipeline step potting, regress row)
NPM hot zone 50.3-57.2 cm3 (was 28 estimated), main zone 62.0-70.6 cm3 (was 58), L4 absorber 28.9 cm3 (inside the 31 cm3 bound); both NPM zones have a fill

### P3 result (src/sim/hood_tolerance.py -> data/results/hood_tolerance.json, pipeline step hood-tol, regress row)
Baseline reproduces the hood v3 gain (+17.1 / +13.1 / +10.4 % at 100 / 200 / 300 mm, upper bound). Field change at 200 mm per mm of uniform placement error,
and the tolerance that keeps the field within 0.5 %:

| Error (all tiles) | Slope | Tolerance for 0.5 % | Consequence for the build |
|---|---|---|---|
| butt gap inner / outer tile | -4.25 % per mm | 0.2 +- 0.12 mm | the critical one: lap or grind the butt faces flat and square, clamp the outer tile against the inner one in the jig, gauge the joint (0.1 / 0.3 mm feelers) |
| layer height | -0.56 % per mm | +-0.9 mm | carrier pocket floor and adhesive layer: a 0.2 mm adhesive film is fine |
| tile thickness | +0.40 % per mm | +-1.25 mm | stock plate tolerance is not a concern (sub-grid thin-layer scaling; see the script) |
| radial position | +0.14 % per mm | +-3.5 mm | pocket clearance can stay generous |
| tile width | +0.03 % per mm | +-16 mm | not a concern |
Not covered: a single misplaced tile breaks the 13-fold symmetry the receive null relies on (3-D model or bench, U-BENCH). Next: the placement jig (generator +
fit check) built around the butt-joint datum, and the bench field measurement with and without tiles (ASSUMPTIONS 8).

### P5 first result (src/sim/fw_timeline.py -> data/results/fw_timeline.json + generated header data/results/pf233_timing.h, pipeline step fw-timeline, regress row)
Per pulse: housekeeping 10 us (ASSUMED), ramp up to 60 us (LUT at the lowest pack voltage), flyback to current zero 3.76 us, blank release 5 us, quiet 5.56 us,
first clean gate 5.91 us (sbq_damping2: RD 680, Cself 100 pF, release 5 us), 9 golden-ratio gates to 449 us. Busy 482 / 500 / 519 us of 1250 / 1667 / 2500 us:
61-79 % of every period is free. Findings:
1. ADC clock: the first gate (5.9-9.6 us) needs SCLK >= 27.2 MHz for 3 conversions; 40 MHz is set as the firmware requirement (AD7389-4 allows 80 MHz);
   the RP2350 PIO / SPI at 40 MHz with the CB1D -> AB1 link is UNVERIFIED (datasheet + scope on the first article).
2. LED refresh: the 66 SK6812-class LEDs need one 2.06 ms unbroken stream (24 bit x 1.25 us each + latch, ASSUMED class timing). It never fits after the
   receive window in any mode, so the firmware drops one or two pulses every 13-27 periods for a 30 Hz display: 5.0-7.7 % fewer pulses. Options: a clocked
   LED type (APA102 class, can pause between chunks, so it fits in the free 61-79 % with no dropped pulse) or a lower display rate (10 Hz: 1.7-2.6 %).
   Owner decision; nothing changed on LB1.
3. The free 61-79 % is room for more late gates (ground balance needs long-tau samples) or a higher PRF in scan: a DSP decision for P6.

## Step 4: summary (15 lines)
1. Exists: parametric generators for six flat-face golden-angle petal formers, the v5 vault cylinder, roof, tray, septum, comb, base plate v3, hood v2, yoke and bridge; 48 curated STLs.
2. Exists: board flows with placement, grid routing, stub fix, independent DRC, Gerbers and KiCad files for LB1, CB1D, SB1R-QA/QB and HTA; netlists and PNG schematics (no .kicad_sch) for the others.
3. Exists: simulations of the TX stage from the real netlists, field and moment, receive leak from the real Gerber copper, damping, thermal, quad bearing, eddy cage; system audits (wiring, stress, stack-up, voxel interference, full head cross-check).
4. Modeled: L 54 uH, 7.96 A m2 at 20 A, Vds 462 V with one 100 nF film clamp, RX-Q leak 0.01-11 mV, first clean gate 5.2-8.3 us, depth about 115/170/205 mm for 0.1/1/5 g gold in free air.
5. Built: nothing. Measured: nothing. No board has been opened in KiCad or a CAM viewer.
6. Riskiest assumption 1: receive leak. The aligned nulls come partly from link radii tuned at 0.0 mm offset (ASSUMPTIONS 5, 13, 15); etch tolerance and layer spacing will set the real leak, and the first gate moves from 5.2 to 8.3 us across the modeled range.
7. Riskiest assumption 2: depth. Gold sphere, step-off, free air, no ground, scaled by a model slope (ASSUMPTIONS 4, 7); mineralised ground usually costs more depth than any modeled gain here.
8. Riskiest assumption 3: parasitics and ratings. Coil self-capacitance (50-350 pF) sets turn-off and ringing (ASSUMPTIONS 1, 14), and every part rating in the stress table is from memory (ASSUMPTIONS 19).
9. First task: the coil damping resistor (OPEN_ITEMS 1).
10. Why: without it the 176 uH receive coils ring for more than 60 us, so every first-gate and depth number above assumes a part that no board carries.
11. It is also small and local: one resistor per coil, a footprint in the SB1R generator, and a re-run of sbq_damp.py with the real input network.
12. Second: CAM/KiCad verification (OPEN_ITEMS 2), because nothing can be ordered until an independent tool agrees with drc.py.
13. Third: the LED rail cap (OPEN_ITEMS 0), a known stress-table failure by design.
14. Housekeeping from step 3: refresh the stale prompts (P01, P02, C04), the check counts and CORRECTIONS row 1; mark the superseded scripts.
15. Nothing above is a measured result; every number is modeled with the assumptions in docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md.
